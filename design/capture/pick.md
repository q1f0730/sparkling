# Pick: V1 — frosted sheet + highlighter reading pass

Shots: `shots/cmp-compose.png`, `shots/cmp-read.png`, `shots/cmp-dev.png` (V1 | V2 | V3, 390x844).

| | V1 Frosted sheet | V2 Index card | V3 Morphing pill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Translucent (his ask) | yes: frosted card, feed shows through | no: opaque paper card on a blurred page | yes |
| Attachments readable | thumbs 60 px + clear 3-icon row | stamp photo, labelled row; ruled lines fight multi-line text | cramped: tray + thumbs + text in one pill |
| Send | round sage icon, bottom-right, under the thumb where the + was | clay seal | small icon inside the pill |
| Reading animation | butter highlighter band sweeps down the print = "someone reading the page"; calm, legible | rows type in, then a loupe wanders: cute but the loupe is hard to see on a busy print | radiating ripples: reads as noise / "processing", no reading metaphor |
| Desktop | same card as a 440 px popover above the + | tilted card looks odd as a popover | a pill floating mid-column has nowhere natural to anchor |

**Why V1:** it is the only one that is genuinely translucent *and* keeps the three capture tools, thumbnails and the send button readable at a glance. Its send button lands exactly where the + was, so open/close is one spatial gesture (spring out of the +, collapse back into it). The highlighter pass is the most honest metaphor for recognition — a pen moving down a printed page — and it stays light, warm and paper-like (no scanline, no glow), which is what he rejected in Arcadia's dark versions. The develop (centre-out, noisy front, sepia warm-up) is shared by all three and stays.

Taken from the others: V2's mono date/eyebrow idea is dropped (noise); V3's "tray" idea is dropped. Kept from Arcadia: ramp `#%*+=~-:,. `, contrast stretch, ink from the photo, 1.7 s develop with smooth mask (no seams).
